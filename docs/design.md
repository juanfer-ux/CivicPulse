## 🔤 Brand
- **Logo:** ring mark plus wordmark (`src/assets/logo-*.svg`, `src/components/Logo.tsx`).
- **Font:** Manrope, served from our own site ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-manrope-font.md)).
- **Theme:** dark only ([decision 0005](decisions/0005-dark-only-figma-tokens.md)).
- **Problems view (owner, 2026-10-30, decision [0030](decisions/0030-problems-above-incidents.md)):** when the database has the problems layer, the map opens on **persistent problems**: one marker each: a rounded tile in the problem status color with the category icon and a count pill of occurrences at its upper right, like the Figma Map Marker (a test in the owner's style; see Problem Marker). **Zoomed out (below zoom 14) a problem is only a circle in its status color, a little bigger than an incident dot** (owner, 2026-10-30), the same idea as incident dots turning into pins; the tile and its number appear from zoom 14. Two tiles that would overlap are not both drawn: the problem with fewer occurrences waits until you zoom in (the closest planted pair, 110 m apart, shows together from about zoom 15.5). A switch next to "Showing n problems" flips to the incident map, unchanged; without the layer, or while an agent answer is on the map, only incidents show. Clicking a problem flies the camera close in (zoom 19, the same GSAP fly with its 3-level cap), opens the **Problem Panel** in the slot of the incident panel and keeps the problem's marker with its number in the middle, with that problem's incidents around it as **tiny dots in the problem's status color** (owner, 2026-10-30: a stack of identical pins is harder to read than dots; the one selected incident still shows its pin); a report in the panel opens the incident panel, whose close button returns to the problem. Escape or a click on empty map closes the incident, then the problem. The filters (locality, category, period) apply to problems, the status chip lists the four problem statuses (Active, Monitoring, Resolved, Archived), and the Stat Cards count problems per locality. Mobile: same, in the bottom sheet.
- **Pointer cursor (owner, 2026-10-30):** everything you can click shows the pointing hand (buttons, menu items, links, selects, checkbox and radio labels); a disabled button keeps the arrow. One rule in `src/index.css` (`@layer base`), because Tailwind 4 no longer sets it on buttons.
- **Incident markers:** (rule, owner 2026-10-26: **the selected incident always shows its pin with the category icon, at any zoom**, even fully zoomed out; layer `incident-selected-pin`, its ring wraps the pin's circle.)
- **Sidebar collapse / expand (test, 2026-10-30):** a quick GSAP tween of the width (0.2 s, `power2.out`, content clipped while it runs; instant with reduced motion); dragging the width does not animate.
- **Closing an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** reverses it: the camera flies back to where the map was before the first incident was opened, and the sidebar expands again if opening the incident was what collapsed it (a sidebar the owner had collapsed stays collapsed).
- **Opening an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** the camera flies to it with GSAP (0.5 to 0.9 s depending on distance, `sine.inOut` (half the peak speed of the first try, and it starts and ends softly), never more than 3 zoom levels animated: a farther trip jumps the rest first, for motion comfort; centre and zoom together, to zoom 17 or the current zoom if closer; instant with reduced motion; touching the map, its zoom buttons or the keyboard stops it). `INCIDENT_ZOOM` in `IncidentMap.tsx`.
- **Incident markers, detail:** small colored circles up to zoom 14 (a flat 6 px across at every zoom below 12, a flat 10 px from zoom 12; the map opens at zoom 12, owner's choice 2026-10-26); from zoom 14 (`BADGE_ZOOM` in `IncidentMap.tsx`) they become map pins (the outline is 2.4 px outside a 32 px pin, 7.5 % of its diameter, Figma Map Pin 2026-10-26; a circle with a short 8 px point whose tip marks the exact spot; 26 px circle growing to 51 px at zoom 18) with the category's icon inside, like Waze: a car for parking, a trash can for illegal dumping (Phosphor, fill weight). Same four status colors; the icons are Phosphor `fill` (Car, Trash), dark, light on Approved. Rings for the agent's answer and the selected incident grow with them. Built in `src/lib/markerIcons.ts`; a new category needs one icon there.
- **Focus:** while an incident is selected, every other dot or pin is toned down toward the land color (40 % of its own color, `KEEP` in `tokens.ts`; same hue, so the status stays readable) and the selected one keeps its full color and is drawn on top; when the agent shows an answer (and nothing is selected) only the answer's incidents stay bright. Clicking an empty part of the map, or pressing Escape, clears the selection and restores everything. (`useEffect` with `fade` in `IncidentMap.tsx`; owner's idea, 2026-10-26.)
- **Basemap:** dark CARTO (OpenStreetMap data). Place and street names are shown in Spanish (the local names, not the English ones CARTO asks for first; owner, 2026-10-25). The credit is required by their licenses, so it stays on the map as a small dark ⓘ that opens on tap (compact MapLibre control, styled in `src/index.css`). Never remove it.

## 📱 Mobile rule
> **Every screen exists in a desktop and a mobile version** (owner's standing rule). Figma frames: desktop **1440×900**, mobile **390×844**.

| | Desktop (1,440 px and wider) | Mobile (under 1,440 px) |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | **Branch `flush-dividers` (test, 2026-10-30): the screen has no padding, the panels touch (no gaps) and have square corners, divided by a 0.5 px `secondary` line on the right of the sidebar, the agent panel and the incident panel; the paragraph below is the rounded version on `main`.** The whole screen has the neutral-300 / neutral-700 / neutral-800 radial gradient as its background (the map is transparent over it) and 12 px of padding on desktop (top, bottom, left and right; owner 2026-10-30) and 4 px between the sidebar and the agent panel (the two sit together in one flex row, no outlines on either; owner 2026-10-30) and 8 px between the agent panel and the map; the sidebar, the agent panel and the map each have 8 px rounded corners. Sidebar on the left; the agent panel docked next to the sidebar at full height (543.5 px), and the map to its right, with no header (removed 2026-10-28): the answer banner and the filters start at the top of the map, and a row of four Stat Cards (the localities with most incidents, each with its trend) above the filters; a row of four Stat Cards (the localities with most incidents) above the filters (everything floating over the map keeps 24 px from its top, left, right and bottom edges; the Stat Cards and the filters are both at most 1,280 px wide and centered in the map area, so on a very wide screen they stop there), and, when an incident is selected, its detail is its own 360 px panel, a column between the agent panel and the map (8 px each side, full height): the map gets narrower instead of being covered, and the sidebar collapses at once to make room (owner 2026-10-30; on phones it stays in the bottom sheet), and a small legend "Showing N incidents" sits at the bottom left (288 px) floating over it; the sidebar starts collapsed | Top bar with a menu button; map with a bottom sheet (Explore / Investigate) |
| Sizes | Body 13, label 14, controls 36 (icon buttons 32) | Body 16, label 16, controls 44 (touch) |

On the sign-in screen the desktop version adds the picture, logo and headline on the left; mobile is the card alone.

The switch is at **1,440 px** (owner's decision): any narrower window, including a half-width laptop browser, gets the mobile layout. In code it is the `desk:` breakpoint in `src/index.css`.

## 🖼️ Figma ↔ code workflow
1. Change the design in Figma (a token, a component, or a screen built from components).
2. Update the code (`src/index.css`, `src/components/ui/*`, then screens) and the [design system](design-system/README.md) in the same change.
3. Check at **1,440** and **390** by **measuring** (heights, radius, text size), not only by eye.
