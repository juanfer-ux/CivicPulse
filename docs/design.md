## 🔤 Brand
- **Logo:** ring mark plus wordmark (`src/assets/logo-*.svg`, `src/components/Logo.tsx`).
- **Font:** Manrope, served from our own site ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-manrope-font.md)).
- **Theme:** dark only ([decision 0005](decisions/0005-dark-only-figma-tokens.md)).
- **One map for problems and incidents (owner, 2026-10-30, decision [0030](decisions/0030-problems-above-incidents.md)):** a **problem** is always a marker (the owner's option F, 2026-11-01, [decision 0032](decisions/0032-problem-marker-option-f.md): a neutral-700 circle with a 1 px outline in the status color, the category icon and a warning triangle in the status color; the circle is 38 px at zoom 12.25 and below and 57 px from zoom 13.9, scaling smoothly in proportion, never replaced by a dot; its radius shows from zoom 11), so problems are easy to tell from incident dots even from far away. **Incidents** stay small circles (and circles with an icon from zoom 13; they were squares until 2026-11-01), but an incident a problem already stands for (a **confirmed** link) is **not drawn**; incidents with no problem yet, and the ones linked only provisionally ("needs review"), still show. Where markers overlap, the one with more occurrences draws on top. Hovering a problem shows the **Problem Card** (occurrences with their dates, reports, interventions) at any zoom. Clicking one flies the camera close in (zoom 18, the map's maximum, same GSAP fly, the whole trip animated with no jump (see Opening an incident below)), opens the **Problem Panel** in the slot of the incident panel (it **waits until the camera has finished flying, then slides out from under the Investigate and Ask panel to the right**, and on closing slides back under it before it disappears; the map no longer shrinks for the panel, it stays under it with camera padding, so no gap shows beside the agent panel, owner 2026-11-01; the incident panel slides the same way, `useSlide` in `src/lib/useSlide.ts`) and keeps the marker in the middle with that problem's incidents around it as **small dots in the problem's status color** (shown for every problem from zoom 14); a report in the panel opens the incident panel, whose close button returns to the problem. Escape or a click on empty map closes the incident, then the problem. **Filters:** locality, category and period apply to both (for a problem, "period" means "reported in that window"); two toggle chips, **Problems 16** and **Incidents 660** (the counts follow the filters), at the bottom left of the map where the legend was (owner, 2026-10-30), choose what the map shows (at least one stays on; **hidden for now since 2026-11-01**, both layers stay on, `SHOW_MAP_TYPE_CHIPS`); one **Status** chip lists the problem statuses (Active, Monitoring, Resolved, Archived) and the incident statuses under two labeled groups, and **only the statuses of what is on**: switch Incidents off and the Status menu has just the problem statuses (and the incident statuses already chosen are cleared). There is no separate legend any more: the counts are in those two chips. The Stat Cards still count incidents (the problem version is not designed yet). While an agent answer is on the map only its incidents show (linked or not). Without the problems layer in the database it is the incident map as before. Mobile: same, in the bottom sheet.
- **Zoom limits (owner, 2026-10-30):** the map zooms in to **18 at most** (MapLibre's default was 22; the basemap has no more detail past zoom 15, so more only stretches it). No minimum is set (MapLibre's default, −2).
- **Pointer cursor (owner, 2026-10-30):** everything you can click shows the pointing hand (buttons, menu items, links, selects, checkbox and radio labels); a disabled button keeps the arrow. One rule in `src/index.css` (`@layer base`), because Tailwind 4 no longer sets it on buttons. The same rule also puts the hand on everything inside a clickable thing (its text, icons and spans), not only on its box, so it cannot drop out over some pixels (owner, 2026-10-30). Select options, tabs, checkboxes and switches (by role) get it too.
- **Incident markers:** (rule, owner 2026-10-26: **the selected incident always shows its pin with the category icon, at any zoom**, even fully zoomed out; layer `incident-selected-pin`, its ring wraps the pin's circle.)
- **Sidebar collapse / expand (test, 2026-10-30):** a quick GSAP tween of the width (0.2 s, `power2.out`, content clipped while it runs; instant with reduced motion); dragging the width does not animate.
- **Closing an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** reverses it: the camera flies back to where the map was before the first incident was opened, and the sidebar expands again if opening the incident was what collapsed it (a sidebar the owner had collapsed stays collapsed). The sidebar changes **1 s after** the camera starts (`SIDEBAR_DELAY_MS` in `Dashboard.tsx`, owner 2026-11-01), so the map does not change width in the first frames of the flight.
- **Opening an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** the camera flies to it with GSAP (0.5 to 0.9 s depending on distance, `sine.inOut` (half the peak speed of the first try, and it starts and ends softly), the whole trip is animated, however far (owner, 2026-11-01: the old jump to 3 levels short felt like starting from somewhere else), with the duration 0.5 s for a short hop up to 1.2 s for the longest; the centre moves in step with the zoom's own growth (2^zoom), so the target drifts steadily toward the middle instead of swinging outward first; to zoom 15 (owner, 2026-10-30; was 17; a problem goes to 18) or the current zoom if closer; instant with reduced motion; touching the map, its zoom buttons or the keyboard stops it). `INCIDENT_ZOOM` in `IncidentMap.tsx`.
- **Incident markers, detail:** see the Incident Marker row in [design-system/components.md](design-system/components.md) (circles, they were squares until 2026-11-01, in 4 size steps by zoom: 8 px below 12, 10 px at 12, 24 px with icon at 13, 30 px with icon from 14; the icon is Phosphor fill, dark, light on Approved; the selected incident gets a circle ring and the camera flies to zoom 15). Built in `src/lib/markerIcons.ts`; a new category needs one icon there.
- **Focus:** while an incident is selected, every other dot or pin is toned down toward the land color (40 % of its own color, `KEEP` in `tokens.ts`; same hue, so the status stays readable) and the selected one keeps its full color and is drawn on top; when the agent shows an answer (and nothing is selected) only the answer's incidents stay bright. Clicking an empty part of the map, or pressing Escape, clears the selection and restores everything. (`useEffect` with `fade` in `IncidentMap.tsx`; owner's idea, 2026-10-26.)
- **Layer order on the map (owner, 2026-10-30; it was text on top):** incidents and problems are always the top layers, over every street and place name. Top to bottom: problem markers, the selected incident's ring and marker, the incident markers and squares, the dots around an open problem, then all the text (the basemap's names, then ours), then the locality highlights and radii, then the rest of the basemap. Set at the end of the layer setup in `IncidentMap.tsx`.
- **Edge smoothing (owner, 2026-10-30):** the map is created with antialiasing on (`canvasContextAttributes: { antialias: true }` in `IncidentMap.tsx`), which smooths the edges of lines, outlines and shapes, and it is drawn at twice the screen's resolution on ordinary 1x screens (`pixelRatio: max(2, devicePixelRatio)`), which the browser shrinks smoothly. Measured on a test map on a 1x screen: the roughness of a problem circle's edge dropped from about 0.55 px to about 0.15 to 0.35 px. Without these the outlines looked crackled. Markers are also drawn as one crisp image per size so no image is shrunk by a fraction. Antialiasing is only on or off (the graphics card picked 4 samples per pixel when measured; MapLibre has no level to set). The problem marker's outline carries a 2 px soft edge (baked into its image) and the radius ring a `circle-blur` of 0.01 (owner, 2026-11-01). The slight blur on roads (0.6 px) and building outlines (2 px) is separate and unchanged.
- **Basemap:** dark CARTO (OpenStreetMap data). Place and street names are shown in Spanish (the local names, not the English ones CARTO asks for first; owner, 2026-10-25). The credit is required by their licenses, so it stays on the map as a small dark ⓘ that opens on tap (compact MapLibre control, styled in `src/index.css`). Never remove it.

| Layout | **Branch `flush-dividers` (test, 2026-10-30): the screen has no padding, the panels touch (no gaps) and have square corners, divided by a 0.5 px `secondary` line on the right of the sidebar, the agent panel and the incident panel; the paragraph below is the rounded version on `main`.** The whole screen has the neutral-400 / neutral-700 / neutral-800 radial gradient (the first stop was neutral-300 until 2026-10-30) as its background (the map is transparent over it) and 12 px of padding on desktop (top, bottom, left and right; owner 2026-10-30) and 4 px between the sidebar and the agent panel (the two sit together in one flex row, no outlines on either; owner 2026-10-30) and 8 px between the agent panel and the map; the sidebar, the agent panel and the map each have 8 px rounded corners. ## 🖼️ Figma ↔ code workflow
1. Change the design in Figma (a token, a component, or a screen built from components).
2. Update the code (`src/index.css`, `src/components/ui/*`, then screens) and the [design system](design-system/README.md) in the same change.
3. Check at **1,440** and **390** by **measuring** (heights, radius, text size), not only by eye.
