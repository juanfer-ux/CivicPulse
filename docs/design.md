## 🔤 Brand
- **Logo:** ring mark plus wordmark (`src/assets/logo-*.svg`, `src/components/Logo.tsx`).
- **Font:** Manrope, served from our own site ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-manrope-font.md)).
- **Theme:** dark only ([decision 0005](decisions/0005-dark-only-figma-tokens.md)).
- **2026-11-05 (owner, decision [0039](decisions/0039-floating-cards-and-timeline-card.md)): the Recurrence and Incident Panels are floating cards in the map's top-left corner and the recurrence's history is two floating cards across the bottom, 12 px apart (24 until 2026-11-07; timeline 60 % left as the anchor, occurrences 40 % right, which scrolls to what you click in the timeline); no slide-out and no camera padding; the camera flight stays.** **One map for recurrences and incidents (owner, 2026-10-30, decision [0030](decisions/0030-problems-above-incidents.md)):** a **recurrence** is always a marker (the owner's option F, 2026-11-01, [decision 0032](decisions/0032-problem-marker-option-f.md): a neutral-700 circle with a 1 px outline in the status color, the category icon and a warning triangle in the status color; the circle is 38 px at zoom 12.25 and below and 57 px from zoom 13.9, scaling smoothly in proportion, never replaced by a dot; its radius shows from zoom 11), so recurrences are easy to tell from incident dots even from far away. **Incidents** stay small circles (and circles with an icon from zoom 13; they were squares until 2026-11-01), but an incident a recurrence already stands for (a **confirmed** link) is **not drawn**; incidents with no recurrence yet, and the ones linked only provisionally ("needs review"), still show. Where markers overlap, the one with more occurrences draws on top. Hovering a recurrence shows the **Recurrence Card** (occurrences with their dates, reports, interventions) at any zoom. Clicking one flies the camera close in (zoom 18, the map's maximum, same GSAP fly, the whole trip animated with no jump (see Opening an incident below)), opens the **Recurrence Panel** as a floating card (it **waits until the camera has finished flying, then appears**; the map never shrinks or pads for it) and keeps the marker in the middle with that recurrence's incidents around it as **small dots in the recurrence's status color** (shown for every recurrence from zoom 14); a report in the panel opens the incident panel, whose close button returns to the problem. Escape or a click on empty map closes the incident, then the problem. **Taps (owner, 2026-11-01):** a recurrence opens only when the tap is on its visible circle or triangle, not in the empty corners of its image, so an incident next to a recurrence can still be tapped; on a touch screen a small box (12 px) around the tap finds the nearest incident dot (`hitsProblemMarker` in `markerSizes.ts`). **A second click on the incident or recurrence that is already open does nothing** (it used to close the panel, owner 2026-11-01); from one of its incidents, a click on the recurrence's marker goes back to the recurrence panel. **Filters:** locality, category and period apply to both (for a recurrence, "period" means "reported in that window"); two toggle chips, **Recurrences 16** and **Incidents 660** (the counts follow the filters), at the bottom left of the map where the legend was (owner, 2026-10-30), choose what the map shows (at least one stays on; **hidden while an incident or recurrence panel is open, back when it closes**, owner 2026-11-01); one **Status** chip lists the recurrence statuses (Active, Monitoring, Resolved, Archived) and the incident statuses under two labeled groups, and **only the statuses of what is on**: switch Incidents off and the Status menu has just the recurrence statuses (and the incident statuses already chosen are cleared). There is no separate legend any more: the counts are in those two chips. The Stat Cards still count incidents (the recurrence version is not designed yet). While an agent answer is on the map only its incidents show (linked or not). Without the recurrences layer in the database it is the incident map as before. Mobile: same, in the bottom sheet.
- **Zoom limits (owner, 2026-10-30):** the map zooms in to **18 at most** (MapLibre's default was 22; the basemap has no more detail past zoom 15, so more only stretches it). No minimum is set (MapLibre's default, −2).
- **Pointer cursor (owner, 2026-10-30):** everything you can click shows the pointing hand (buttons, menu items, links, selects, checkbox and radio labels); a disabled button keeps the arrow. One rule in `src/index.css` (`@layer base`), because Tailwind 4 no longer sets it on buttons. The same rule also puts the hand on everything inside a clickable thing (its text, icons and spans), not only on its box, so it cannot drop out over some pixels (owner, 2026-10-30). Select options, tabs, checkboxes and switches (by role) get it too.
- **Incident markers:** (rule, owner 2026-10-26: **the selected incident always shows its pin with the category icon, at any zoom**, even fully zoomed out; layer `incident-selected-pin`, its ring wraps the pin's circle.)
- **Sidebar collapse / expand (test, 2026-10-30):** a GSAP tween of the width (0.32 s since 2026-11-08, it was 0.2 s; `power2.out`, content clipped while it runs; instant with reduced motion); dragging the width does not animate.
- **Closing an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** reverses it: the camera flies back to where the map was before the first incident was opened, and the sidebar expands again if opening the incident was what collapsed it (a sidebar the owner had collapsed stays collapsed). The sidebar changes **1 s after** the camera starts (`SIDEBAR_DELAY_MS` in `Dashboard.tsx`, owner 2026-11-01), so the map does not change width in the first frames of the flight.
- **Opening an incident (test, 2026-10-30):** the camera flies to it with GSAP (0.5 to 0.9 s depending on distance, `sine.inOut` (half the peak speed of the first try, and it starts and ends softly), the whole trip is animated, however far (owner, 2026-11-01: the old jump to 3 levels short felt like starting from somewhere else), with the duration 0.5 s for a short hop up to 1.2 s for the longest; the centre moves in step with the zoom's own growth (2^zoom), so the target drifts steadily toward the middle instead of swinging outward first; to zoom 15 (owner, 2026-10-30; was 17; a recurrence goes to 18) or the current zoom if closer; instant with reduced motion; touching the map, its zoom buttons or the keyboard stops it). `INCIDENT_ZOOM` in `IncidentMap.tsx`.
- **Incident markers, detail:** see the Incident Marker row in [design-system/components.md](design-system/components.md) (circles, they were squares until 2026-11-01, in 4 size steps by zoom: 8 px below 12, 10 px at 12, 24 px with icon at 13, 30 px with icon from 14; the icon is Phosphor fill, dark, light on Approved; the selected incident gets a circle ring and the camera flies to zoom 15). Built in `src/lib/markerIcons.ts`; a new category needs one icon there.
- **Focus:** while an incident is selected, every other dot or pin is toned down toward the land color (40 % of its own color, `KEEP` in `tokens.ts`; same hue, so the status stays readable) and the selected one keeps its full color and is drawn on top; when the agent shows an answer (and nothing is selected) only the answer's incidents stay bright. Clicking an empty part of the map, or pressing Escape, clears the selection and restores everything. (`useEffect` with `fade` in `IncidentMap.tsx`; owner's idea, 2026-10-26.)
- **Layer order on the map (owner, 2026-10-30; it was text on top):** incidents and recurrences are always the top layers, over every street and place name. Top to bottom: recurrence markers, the selected incident's ring and marker, the incident markers and squares, the dots around an open recurrence, then all the text (the basemap's names, then ours), then the locality highlights and radii, then the rest of the basemap. Set at the end of the layer setup in `IncidentMap.tsx`.
- **Edge smoothing (owner, 2026-10-30):** the map is created with antialiasing on (`canvasContextAttributes: { antialias: true }` in `IncidentMap.tsx`), which smooths the edges of lines, outlines and shapes, and it is drawn at twice the screen's resolution on ordinary 1x screens (`pixelRatio: max(2, devicePixelRatio)`), which the browser shrinks smoothly. Measured on a test map on a 1x screen: the roughness of a recurrence circle's edge dropped from about 0.55 px to about 0.15 to 0.35 px. Without these the outlines looked crackled. Markers are also drawn as one crisp image per size so no image is shrunk by a fraction. Antialiasing is only on or off (the graphics card picked 4 samples per pixel when measured; MapLibre has no level to set). The recurrence marker's outline carries a 2 px soft edge (baked into its image) and the radius ring a `circle-blur` of 0.01 (owner, 2026-11-01). The slight blur on roads (0.6 px) and building outlines (2 px) is separate and unchanged.
- **Basemap:** dark CARTO (OpenStreetMap data). Place and street names are shown in Spanish (the local names, not the English ones CARTO asks for first; owner, 2026-10-25). The credit is required by their licenses, so it stays on the map as a small dark ⓘ that opens on tap (compact MapLibre control, styled in `src/index.css`). Never remove it.

## 🖼️ Figma ↔ code workflow
1. Change the design in Figma (a token, a component, or a screen built from components).
2. Update the code (`src/index.css`, `src/components/ui/*`, then screens) and the [design system](design-system/README.md) in the same change.
3. **Figma screens are auto layout from the root to the deepest frame** (owner, 2026-11-12): the screen frame itself has auto layout, every container inside it does too, children use Fill / Hug / Fixed on purpose, so the screen reflows when its size changes. Only vector logos and illustrations made of paths, and elements deliberately set to absolute position, are exempt. All Screens frames follow it since 2026-11-12 (map placeholders, which are decorative illustrations, excepted). **When a screen changes size, nothing may be left empty (owner, 2026-11-12):** after resizing or converting a screen, every container and component must grow to fill the new space (Fill width or height where it should stretch, Hug where it should not), sidebars and panels fill the screen height, side-by-side areas share the width (one Fill, the others Hug or Fixed), decorative backgrounds such as the map placeholder are extended to the full frame, and floating overlays are anchored (left, right or stretch) so they follow their edge. Then compare the screenshot edge to edge, looking for empty strips at the right and bottom, overlaps and clipped content. **"Ignore auto layout" (absolute position) is only for background layers (owner, 2026-11-12), like the map placeholder.** Anything that sits on top of a background (panels, cards, legends, a detail panel) must not be absolute: put it inside an auto-layout box (for example a Map_Area frame with padding, Fill width and height, and alignment such as top-right) so it stays structured and follows the screen.
4. Check at **1,880 × 1,000** by **measuring** (heights, radius, text size), not only by eye.
